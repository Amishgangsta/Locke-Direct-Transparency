## Representative workflows

- **Free:** create a Motor Vehicle Bill of Sale from a focused known-facts form.
- **Customized:** prepare a residential lease with a structured interview and selected terms.
- **Advanced:** explore will-specific decisions or transaction contingencies through deeper questions.
- **Complete Package:** coordinate multiple related estate-planning documents with a role-aware interview.

Locke Direct is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not guarantee a particular document's legal sufficiency for every situation.
