# Why choose Locke Direct? — Market context

**Last reviewed: November 13, 2026.** This is a comparison of vendors' published offerings, not a measured legal-quality ranking or a claim that other services lack every feature listed here.

## Locke Direct's differentiators

1. **Transparent, fixed, one-time pricing:** free self-guided forms, $14.99 customized agreements, $24.99 advanced documents, and $49.99 coordinated packages; no subscription required.
2. **Explained choices:** the interview explains a term's practical consequences and which interests it protects, rather than merely collecting names and dates.
3. **A carefully bounded AI assistant:** Ask Locke helps customers navigate the catalog and understand their fields, while document text follows maintained clauses and rule-based assembly.
4. **Depth matched to the need:** customers can choose focused self-service, more extensive guided questioning, or packages coordinating multiple documents.
5. **Privacy-oriented separation:** public marketing analytics are consent-gated and excluded from private document-preparation routes.
